# Rock Cowboy Zombie Survival

You're Mr. Rock Cowboy. The zombies never stop coming. Survive as long as you can, rack up kills, and unlock a huge arsenal along the way.

**Play it in your browser:** [macman000.github.io/rockcowboyzombie](https://macman000.github.io/rockcowboyzombie/)

## How to play

**Desktop**

| Action | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Move | `W` `A` `S` `D` or the arrow keys |
| Aim | Mouse |
| Shoot / attack | Hold left click |
| Special ability (some weapons) | `E` |
| Pause | `Esc` or the pause button |
| Change weapon | Backpack button (top left) |

**Phone / tablet**

Use the on-screen arrows to move. The **SHOOT** button fires and auto-aims at the nearest zombie. The **E** button triggers a weapon's special ability. Tap the backpack to switch weapons.

## Tips

- You start with just the Pistol. Every **20 kills** unlocks the next weapon, so keep killing.
- The bar at the top shows your progress to the next unlock.
- You have **10 hits** of health. Watch your health bar.
- Every weapon has **infinite ammo**, but many have **cooldowns**, so pick the right tool for the moment.
- Some weapons do more than shoot: throw things, place mines and turrets, freeze or stun zombies, call down meteors, and more. Check the info under your gun name to see cooldowns and what `E` does.
- A **horde** of extra zombies arrives when the countdown in the top right hits zero. Be ready.
- Not every zombie is the same. Watch out for the tiny fast ones, the red ones that explode, and the big slow ones.
- Something big waits for the most dedicated survivors...
- There's a secret-code menu hidden in the pause screen. See what you can find.

Your progress is **not saved**. Every run starts fresh.

## Offline play

Want to play without an internet connection? Download `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser. The game is a single HTML file. No installation or extra files required.

## Version updates

The current release is **v8**. Check back occasionally for a newer version. When the version number changes, delete your old `index.html` and download the latest one to stay up to date.
